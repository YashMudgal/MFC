Your Trusted Facility Maintenance Partner

A Decade of Asset Protection & Excellence

For over a decade, Minnekhada Facilities Consulting has been delivering reliable, high-quality property maintenance solutions to a diverse range of clients throughout Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley. Our experience includes serving provincial government facilities, municipalities, school districts, commercial properties, and private property owners, providing dependable services that protect and enhance the value of their assets.

Our comprehensive maintenance services include:

  • Duct cleaning
  • Window cleaning
  • Janitorial services
  • Carpet cleaning
  • Asphalt paving & repairs
  • Parking lot line painting
  • Pressure washing

Our experienced team delivers prompt, professional, and cost-effective solutions tailored to the unique needs of every property.
